Privacy policy.

Information we receive

We receive the contact and project information you choose to send by our inquiry form, email, WhatsApp or other direct channels, including drawings and reference material needed to review a scope.

How we use it

Information is used to respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, coordinate work and deliver agreed services. We do not sell personal information.

Website inquiries

The form sends your company name, email, service choice, estimated quantity, target date, project description and source page to our business email through our email provider. We use this information to respond to your request. Avoid including confidential files or passwords.

For spam prevention, the form service keeps a hashed network identifier and request times for a one-hour rate-limit window; expired records are removed on the next submission. Inquiry content is not stored by the form service; email correspondence is retained for project communication and can be reviewed through the contact below.

Optional analytics

Inquiry clicks, successful form submissions, email clicks, WhatsApp clicks and source page paths may be measured only when analytics is configured and your consent allows it. Form contents and email addresses are not sent in these events. Google Analytics is loaded only when configured and after you accept optional analytics. You can reject analytics without losing access to the inquiry form. Use Privacy choices to change your decision; withdrawing consent disables analytics and reloads the page. We remember your choice in this browser for up to 180 days. Analytics cookies, when accepted, are configured for up to 180 days. Browser privacy signals (Global Privacy Control) keep optional analytics disabled. Query strings, URL fragments and referring URLs are excluded from our configured events. Google receives technical connection information when its service loads; see Google’s privacy policy.

Project files

Send only material you are authorized to share. Public portfolio samples are anonymized; confidential project material is not published without permission.
